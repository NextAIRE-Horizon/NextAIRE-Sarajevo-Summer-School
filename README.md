# NextAIRE Sarajevo Summer School

Welcome to the official repository of the **NextAIRE Sarajevo Summer School in Next-Generation Air Quality**.

The Summer School kicked off on **7 September 2026** at the **University of Sarajevo – Faculty of Science**, bringing together researchers, project partners and experts for **three days of learning, knowledge exchange and collaboration**.

It is organised by the **University of Sarajevo – Faculty of Science, Department of Chemistry**, together with the **Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Computer Science and Information Technology Osijek (FERIT)**.

## About the NextAIRE Project

NextAIRE combines scientific knowledge, practical training, AI skills, transferable skills and international collaboration to support the **next generation of researchers working on air quality**. The Summer School is one of the project's key training and capacity-building activities.

## Programme Overview

### Day 1 – Air Quality Monitoring, Sensors and Environmental Intelligence

The first day opened with welcome remarks from representatives of the University of Sarajevo and the NextAIRE project, followed by keynote lectures addressing important challenges and new approaches in air-quality research:

- Air Quality Monitoring in Bosnia and Herzegovina: Current Challenges and Future Prospects
- From aerosol chemistry to environmental policy: Introduction to receptor modelling for PM source apportionment
- Urban new particle formation and atmospheric parameters

The afternoon continued with a full programme of hard-skills sessions covering fundamental and practical aspects of modern air-quality monitoring, including **particulate matter, air-quality measurements, sensor reliability, low-cost sensors supported by machine learning, environmental intelligence, air-quality management, IoT-based environmental monitoring, mobile measurements, air-quality mapping and data interpretation**. The day also included a guided tour of the Faculty and its research laboratories.

### Day 2 – Artificial Intelligence, Data Tools, Co-Creation and Soft Skills

The second day focused on **Artificial Intelligence, practical data tools, co-creation and transferable skills**:

- **Hard-skills sessions:** Introduction to Neural Networks (Antonio Antunović and Mislav Ćaleta – FERIT), NextAIRE Data Platform hands-on session (Alexander Hiebl and Heimo Gursch – Know Center Research GmbH), AI Challenges (Dr Sead Delalić – Olden Labs / University of Sarajevo – Faculty of Science), and *The Model That Never Saw Your Data: Modern AI Tools for Air-Quality Time Series* (Prof. Emmanuel Karlo Nyarko – FERIT)
- **Co-creation activity:** a dedicated session on the **NextAIRE Competency Framework**, gathering participants' perspectives on the knowledge and skills needed by researchers working with Artificial Intelligence and air-quality challenges
- **Soft-skills sessions:** Product Development Lifecycle (Zvonimir Anic – Smart Sense), a session by Dr Lejla Gurbeta Pokvić (Director – Verlab Institute), *Nobody Buys an R²: Turning Research into Product(s)* (Dejan Strbad – Ascalia / Kraken), Science-to-Policy Translation (Jon Switters – The Lisbon Council), and Intercultural Cooperation within the Western Balkan Countries (Prof. Dr Damir Marjanović – Institute for Anthropological Research)
- **Networking:** the day concluded with a networking event, dinner and guided tour of Sarajevo

### Day 3

The final day concluded the Summer School programme. Its presentations are **not included** in this repository.
